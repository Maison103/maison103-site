Capability Narrative
Our Approach

Maison 103 LLC is a Minority Business Enterprise (MBE) headquartered in Miramar, Florida, specializing in analytics, management consulting, and program support for federal and state agencies.

We bring deep expertise in data analytics, AI, business intelligence, and process improvement — translating complex operational data into actionable insights that drive mission outcomes.

We are actively targeting the VA, HHS, DoD, DHS, and the Department of the Treasury with scalable solutions designed for each agency's requirements and compliance frameworks.

Maison 103 operates with a commitment to precision, accountability, and measurable results. We integrate seamlessly with existing teams to accelerate delivery and enhance performance.
